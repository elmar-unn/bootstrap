About the Game

A quick glance at what the game is about

You start at Limgrave where you are met by a mysterious man who tells you to find a guide for your journey. After lighting 3 sites of grace you are accompanied by Melina who will be your guide for the rest of the game.

You must head to Stormveil castle to defeat Godrick the Grafted but are ambushed by Margit the fell Omen. Margit goes down and you continue foward to get the first great rune from Godrick.

After claiming the first great rune you are ready to explore Liurnia of the Lakes where you will find a key to Raya Lucaria Academy, a school of wizards.

You are given a choice to either go to Raya Lucaria to beat Rennala of The Full Moon. Or head to Caelid to defeat general Radahn.

After claiming your second great rune the gates of Leyndell the capital will be open. There is however a tree sentinel guarding the door who you have to defeat.

After many incidents happened outside of the capital the rune of destined death was claimed and the erdtree was put aflame the capital became of ashes.

After lighting the erdtree you are met by Godfrey the First Elden Lord who will attempt to stop you from becoming Elden Lord. After defeating him you must also beat Radagon of The Golden Order to claim The Elden Ring.
